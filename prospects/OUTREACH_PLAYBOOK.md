# Outreach playbook — prospect lists A–D

**Nothing on these lists has been contacted.** Before anyone calls or visits, Isaac approves:
(1) the list, (2) the person working it, and (3) that the person is properly engaged (classification, pay, agreement and training — see `docs/NOVA_SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`).

## Before the first call (every number)
1. Check the number against the **National Do Not Call Registry** and against Nova's own suppression list in the CRM, and record the check. How far business-to-business calls are exempt is a question for Nova's counsel; until it is answered, treat every number as needing the check.
2. If the business has asked any Nova person not to contact it, mark **Do not contact** in the CRM and stop.
3. **Phone calls only.** A published business phone is **not** consent to text or email. Do not text, do not add anyone to an email list, do not use automated or prerecorded calls.

## Calling (Lists A–C)
- Call during the business's normal hours, never before 9:00 or after 17:00 local time; avoid Monday mornings for trades.
- One call attempt plus at most one voicemail per week, three attempts in total; then mark *No response* and stop.
- Use the row's **opener**. It is a question, not a claim. Never tell a business it is "losing" customers — we have not examined their records.
- The goal of a first call is **one** of: a short discovery conversation, permission to run a Nova Audit, or a clear "no". Offer the Audit only as described in the approved claims sheet.
- Record every outcome in the CRM the same day (`log-activity`). "Not interested" → close the lead. "Don't call again" → mark do-not-contact.

## Walk-ins (List D)
- Visit only during the listed hours and inside the suggested window. Re-check the hours source the day before.
- Public customer areas only. Never go behind a counter, into a service bay, kitchen, grooming area or any staff-only space.
- If the owner is busy or absent: leave a card and go. No appointment exists unless the business made one with you.
- Keep it under two minutes unless they ask questions.

## Public observations
Each row may list something visible on the business's own website (for example, a "Book Now" button that leads to a contact form). These are **conversation starters, not findings**. Ask: "I noticed X — is that how you prefer requests to come in?" Never present an observation as proof of lost revenue.

## Importing into the CRM
Owner → Sales Team → Leads → Import, paste a `crm_import_list_*.csv`, choose the assignee. The import skips duplicates and never creates consent. The CSVs contain no commas inside values because the import splits on commas.
