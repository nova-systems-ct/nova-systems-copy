# Nova: product definition (goals, not status)

Source: Isaac Nova's repair brief of 2026-10-25. This file states **what Nova is meant to be**. It does not say
what is built. Implemented and verified behavior lives in `docs/NOVA_OPERATIONAL_MVP_CHECKPOINT.md`; an aspiration
here is never a feature claim.

## Identity
Nova Systems LLC (Waterbury, CT; owner Isaac Nova) is an adaptable business-systems company with a high-end
experience. Nova finds where a business loses opportunities, money and time, installs systems that do the work,
and measures the result. It is not a website agency, an AI demo, a set of dashboards, or a one-industry template.
A website, chatbot, receptionist or video is a possible component, never the definition.

## Products (naming authority: the approved product catalog; invent no new pillars or prices)
- **Nova Audit (the constant).** Public research plus authorized business inputs, process observation and, when
  appropriate, an in-person visit. Covers attract → respond → qualify → book/quote → agree → collect → deliver →
  follow up → retain, plus wasted subscriptions, disconnected tools, handoffs and industry constraints. Every finding
  carries evidence, confidence, known metrics and missing inputs. Losses are quantified only when data supports it.
  The 48–72 h promise needs an owner-approved clock policy and complete inputs. The 5-minute public demo is a scoped
  sample of the real engine, not a replacement. An in-person process audit is not a building/safety inspection.
- **Nova Agents.** Workers that finish tasks on supported connected channels: capture, keep context, answer within
  approved rules, collect missing details, qualify, offer real availability, prepare the agreement, book, confirm,
  hand off. Deposits go through the client's own payment setup; client money is never Nova revenue.
- **Nova Revive.** Recover stalled inquiries, quotes and bookings; re-engage eligible past customers with consent,
  suppression and stop-on-reply. Show impact from real records only.
- **Nova Demand.** Create qualified opportunities through researched offers, prospecting, content, creative,
  partnerships and measurable, owner-approved outreach. No guaranteed customer counts.
- **Background responsibilities:** monitoring, security, escalation, reporting, capacity. The hierarchy shows
  owner → coordinator roles → specialist workers, and only roles with real executable capability appear active.

Each channel (phone, forms, email, SMS, WhatsApp, social, Apple Messages/Wallet) needs a real integration,
permissions and proof. Never imply a channel is live when it is not.

## Delivery model: audit-driven installation
1. Read approved findings, business profile, outcomes, rules, current integrations.
2. Recommend compatible catalog modules with purpose, inputs, permissions, dependencies, ongoing cost. No auto-approval.
3. Owner connects existing tools via scoped credentials/OAuth and configures services, availability, prices, documents,
   escalation contacts. No production secrets in chat.
4. Preview plan, permissions, external effects and unsupported needs; get authorization at that boundary.
5. Provision tested modules idempotently, logged, versioned, recoverable.
6. Run isolated acceptance checks; show installed / blocked / disabled / failed with a next step.
7. Record versions, config, evidence, monitoring; support update and disable/uninstall without erasing client records.
New requirements become tested reusable modules before entering the catalog. Prefer the client's existing system of
record; make sync direction, dedupe, webhooks, reconciliation and recovery explicit.

7. Requests/inbox, training, remaining sections. 8. Browser/data/security verification, one clean final run.

## Hard limits
No purchases, subscription changes, real messages, charges, production publishes, public tunnels or production data
changes without the owner. No fabricated findings, metrics, revenue, workers or activity. Simulated or local-adapter
results are never reported as live-provider results.
