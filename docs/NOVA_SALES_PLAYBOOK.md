# Nova sales playbook (draft for Isaac's approval, 2026-10-13)

**Status:** draft. Nothing here may be used with a prospect until Isaac approves it. Salespeople may not
work until their classification, compensation, written agreement, training and permission are settled;
no unpaid arrangement is assumed lawful. A prospect list is preparation, not permission to call, text or email.

## What Nova is (say it this way)
Nova Systems is an operations firm for local service businesses. We help a business respond to inquiries,
book appointments, send its approved agreement, collect deposits, follow up with past customers who agreed
to hear from it, and check every week that it all keeps working. We are not an advertising agency and not
just a chatbot. Our work sits on the handoffs between departments; it does not replace a business's IT,
marketing or bookkeeping.

## 20-second introduction
"I'm ___ with Nova Systems in Waterbury. We work with local service businesses on the part between a customer
reaching out and a booked, paid job — replying, scheduling, agreements, deposits, follow-up. We usually start
with a short audit of how inquiries are handled today, and you decide what, if anything, to change."

## Openers
- **Cold call:** "Hi, this is ___ with Nova Systems here in Waterbury. Is this the owner? … I'll be quick — when someone
  calls or fills in your website form while your team is busy, what usually happens to that request?" (Then use the row's opener.)
- **Networking:** "What kind of work does your business do? … How do most of your new customers first get in touch with you?"
- **Walk-in:** "Hi, I'm ___ with Nova Systems in Waterbury. I won't take more than a minute — is the owner or manager in? If not, may I leave a card?"

## Discovery questions (listen; do not diagnose)
1. How do new customers reach you — phone, website form, text, walk-in?
2. Who answers them, and what happens after hours or when everyone is on a job?
3. How does a request become a scheduled appointment? Who sends prices, agreements, deposits?
4. Do you follow up with past customers? Have they agreed to hear from you?
5. How would you know if requests were going unanswered?
6. What tools do you already use for scheduling, invoicing and payments?
7. What would have to be true for a change to be worth your time?

## "We already have IT / marketing / a bookkeeper"
"Good — we don't replace them. Nova looks at the handoffs between them: the inquiry that marketing produced,
the reply, the booking, the agreement, the deposit. If those already run smoothly, the audit will say so and
you'll have spent very little to confirm it."

## Explaining the Nova Audit
- We agree in writing what we will look at, the timing, and anything we may test. The 48–72 hour window starts
  only once your information is in and the plan is approved.
- We review public information (website, listings, contact paths). Calls or forms to your business are tested
  **only with your written permission**; we never pose as a fake emergency and never contact your competitors' customers.
- The report separates what we observed from what we estimate, and shows the source of every number. Money
  estimates are labeled as estimates; only figures from your own records are called verified.
- Each recommendation says whether Nova can implement it. You decide.

## Objections
- **"Send me something."** "Happy to — what address should I send it to?" Send the brochure only after Isaac approves it (`collateral/`); until then, offer a short call instead. Only email when they give the address and ask for it.
- **"How much?"** "Isaac quotes after a short conversation, so it fits what you actually need. Can I set up 15 minutes?"
- **"We're too busy."** "That's usually when requests slip. Would a 15-minute call next week be easier?"
- **"Is this AI?"** "Some parts use automation, always with your prices and your approval, and anything unusual goes to a person. The phone assistant isn't live yet."
- **"Guarantee results?"** "No. We measure and report what actually happens, including what we can't measure."
- **"Not interested."** "Understood — thank you. Should I note that you'd rather not hear from us again?" Record it.

## Follow-up (only after a conversation, by the channel the prospect chose)
"Thanks for talking with me today, ___. As promised, here is [what they asked for]. If a short Nova Audit would be useful,
reply here and Isaac will follow up with scope and price. — ___, Nova Systems"

## Escalate to Isaac immediately when
Pricing, discounts or contract terms come up; a prospect asks for anything in writing about results; legal,
medical, insurance or data-privacy questions; a complaint; a request not to be contacted (record it first);
any healthcare or regulated business; any request to text or email lists of customers.

## Approved claims
- Nova is based in Waterbury, Connecticut, and works with local service businesses.
- The Nova Audit reviews public information and, only with written permission, tests how inquiries are answered.
- Reports separate observation from estimate and cite sources.
- Automation uses only the business's approved prices and agreement, and hands unusual or upset customers to a person.
- Deposits are taken on Stripe's page; Nova never sees card numbers.
- Opt-outs are honored immediately.

## Not approved — never say
"Guaranteed", "100%", any revenue or ROI number, "you're losing $X", testimonials or client names, prices,
"AI receptionist is live", "we're HIPAA compliant", "fully secure", comparisons that call another company worse,
and anything about competitors' customers.
