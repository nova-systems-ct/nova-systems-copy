Service text terms.

Nova Systems service-text program

This program covers automated messages about a service request you initiate with Nova Systems, such as requested booking links, appointment reminders and service updates. We provide business technology and automation services. Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment, price or contract.

How you join

On the service-request form, enter your phone number and independently select the optional service-text checkbox before submitting. The checkbox starts unchecked. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Providing a phone number or submitting a form without selecting that box does not authorize texts. The program does not include promotional messages.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's plan determines any charges to you. Message delivery depends on carrier availability; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Opting out ends automated service texts from this program, apart from a permitted opt-out confirmation. You may also contact isaac@nova-systems.app or call (203) 706-0504. Restarting service texts does not authorize promotional messages.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We store your request and the exact SMS disclosure and choice to document what you authorized.
