Service request privacy.

This policy covers Nova Systems' service-request form and optional service-text program. Nova Systems provides business technology and automation services in Waterbury, Connecticut.

Information we collect

We collect the name, business name, email, optional phone number and request details you submit. We store the selected SMS choice, its service-only scope, the exact disclosure and version, source page, request reference and server-recorded time. We use these records to handle your request and document your messaging permission.

Optional service texts

Selecting the separate unchecked service-text box authorizes automated texts about that request, including requested booking links, appointment reminders and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Providing a phone number, requesting help or accepting general policies alone does not grant SMS permission. This checkbox does not grant promotional-text permission. Leaving it unchecked does not prevent a request or purchase.

No sharing for marketing

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process this information only to store the request, operate authorized services or deliver the messages you requested, under their obligations to protect it.

Your choices

Reply STOP to opt out of service texts or HELP for help. Contact isaac@nova-systems.app to request access, correction or deletion of your information or to withdraw permission. We keep records as needed to handle requests, provide agreed services and document opt-in or opt-out choices.
