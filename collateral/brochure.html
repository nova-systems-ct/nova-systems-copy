How a Nova Audit works

  1. You tell us what to look at. Your services, hours and how customers reach you.
  2. We agree the timing. The 48–72 hour window starts only once your inputs are in and the plan is approved.
  3. We review and, with your written permission, test. Public website and listings; calls and forms only where you authorize them. We never pose as a fake emergency.
  4. You get a written report. What we observed is kept separate from what we estimate, and every number shows its source.
  5. You decide. Each recommendation says whether Nova can implement it. Nothing changes without your approval.

INSIDE · panel 1

What Nova does

Four parts, one job: fewer missed customers.

Nova Audit

Find out what happens to your inquiries

We review your public website, listings and contact paths and, with your written permission, test how calls and forms are answered. You get a report that separates what we observed from what we estimate, with the source of every number.

Delivered within an agreed 48–72 hour window once your inputs are in.

Nova Core

Answer, qualify and book — with your prices

Customers who text or fill in your form get a prompt reply, choose a service from your approved price list, pick a real open time, sign your agreement and pay a deposit through Stripe. The assistant never invents prices or discounts.

Anything unusual, upset or urgent goes to a person on your team.

INSIDE · panel 2

Nova Harvest

Follow up on the customers you already have

A missed call can get one text back where the caller has given permission. Past customers who agreed to hear from you can be invited back, after you approve the list, the message and the schedule. Feedback requests are neutral and go to every customer.

Opt-outs are honored immediately, including messages already queued.

Nova Sentry

Know it is working — every week

We monitor the system's health and alert a person when something fails. Every Sunday evening you get a short report of inquiries, bookings, signed agreements and confirmed deposits, built from recorded events.

If something cannot be measured, the report says so instead of guessing.

What we do not do

We do not run ads, buy contact lists, or text anyone who has not given permission. We never invent prices, reviews or results, and we do not change anything in your business without your approval.

INSIDE · panel 3 (back of flap)

How we work

A person is always in the loop

When a customer is upset, asks for a person, needs a custom quote, or the system is unsure, the conversation goes to your team and automation pauses. The assistant does not handle emergencies or give medical or legal advice.

Your customers' data

Each business's records are kept separate. Signed agreements open through short-lived private links. Card details are entered on Stripe's page, never given to Nova. We do not sell data.
